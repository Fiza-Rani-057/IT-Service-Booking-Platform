=========First Block===========

1. querySelectorAll()

 Function: Multiple elements select karta hai.
 Example:const buttons = document.querySelectorAll("button");

 2. forEach()

 Function: Selected items par one-by-one kaam karta hai.
 Example:buttons.forEach(function(button) {
    button.style.display = "none";
});

 3. window.scrollTo
 Function: Page ko kisi specific position par le jata hai.

 window.scrollTo({
    top: 0,
    behavior: "smooth"
});
